1. Information We Collect

We respect player privacy. Color Swap Dodger does not collect or store sensitive personal information such as your real name, address, or phone number. However, the app integrates standard third-party services that may collect automated technical device information to deliver game services:

  • Google Play Games & Firebase Authentication: When you opt to sign in with Google, we process your public display name, avatar photo URL, and high scores to manage global online leaderboards.

  • Local Device Storage: Your unlocked skins, star currency, audio preferences, and high scores are saved locally on your mobile device.

Since game progress and high scores are stored on your device and synced via Google Play Games, you may delete your local data at any time by clearing the application data or uninstalling the app. To request removal of your leaderboard handle, email us at mahethekiller@gmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy Protection

Color Swap Dodger does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal information, please contact us for immediate removal.
